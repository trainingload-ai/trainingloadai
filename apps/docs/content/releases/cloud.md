## 2026-11-20

### en

- Fixed an issue that could leave imported Intervals.icu activities stuck during analysis.
- Today’s AI training suggestions now identify the workout already on your schedule or explain why an optional extra session fits today.
- Updated the recovery charts on the web to show the latest daily readings and use consistent date ranges for trends.

### zh-Hans

- 修复了 Intervals.icu 导入的活动可能卡在分析阶段的问题。
- AI 教练推荐今日训练时，会指出对应的已排训练，或解释为什么今天适合安排一堂可选的加练。
- 更新了网页版恢复图表，显示最近的每日读数，并统一趋势的日期范围。

## 2026-11-19

### en

- Continuous training plans can now generate future weeks as your plan progresses.
- The AI coach now checks the selected time window when suggesting a workout for today.
- When creating an extra workout, the AI coach shows a preview for you to confirm before adding it to your schedule.

### zh-Hans

- 持续训练计划现在可以随着训练推进，自动生成后续周的安排。
- AI 教练推荐今日训练时，会检查你选择的可训练时段。
- 用 AI 教练新增一堂训练时，可以先预览内容，确认后再加到日程。
