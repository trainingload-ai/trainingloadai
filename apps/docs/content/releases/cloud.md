## 2026-11-19

### en

- Continuous training plans can now generate future weeks as your plan progresses.
- The AI coach now checks the selected time window when suggesting a workout for today.
- When creating an extra workout, the AI coach shows a preview for you to confirm before adding it to your schedule.

### zh-Hans

- 持续训练计划现在可以随着训练推进，自动生成后续周的安排。
- AI 教练推荐今日训练时，会检查你选择的可训练时段。
- 用 AI 教练新增一堂训练时，可以先预览内容，确认后再加到日程。
