# FormBeat alternative for training load

**TL;DR:** FormBeat is a cycling analytics product with fitness / fatigue / form, power-curve tools, and an AI coach that can propose the next ride from recent load. Trainingload.ai is a FormBeat alternative when you want that same conversation **inside an active plan** for **running and cycling**, with adjustments you confirm. Stay on FormBeat if you are a cyclist who mainly wants Strava-linked daily suggestions.

This page is for people comparing FormBeat.com with a training load app that treats the calendar as the object being changed, not only the next recommended session.

## Why people look for a FormBeat alternative

Typical triggers:

- The athlete also runs or races triathlon and does not want a cycling-only analysis home.
- Daily AI suggestions are useful, but the weekly plan still has to absorb missed intervals, travel, or a rising ATL.
- They want PMC-style language next to **plan vs completed**, not only next to yesterday's ride.
- They already use TrainingPeaks or Intervals.icu for planning and are deciding whether a second AI cycling layer is enough.

Those are product-fit questions. FormBeat is not "too simple"; it is aimed at a narrower sport surface.

## Where FormBeat is especially strong

From FormBeat's public product and help content (reviewed September 2026):

- **Cycling-native analytics.** Power curve, Critical Power and W', FTP detection, intensity / decoupling, and interval structure are first-class.
- **Fitness, fatigue, and form.** A performance-management style chart plus form prediction for events — the same family of questions athletes ask of a PMC.
- **AI coach on ride history.** Morning suggestions can weigh recent load and recovery, including rest, and you can ask pacing or priority questions against your power history.
- **Ingest path.** Strava and Garmin connections, indoor platforms that already flow through Strava, plus FIT / TCX / GPX upload.
- **Public pricing (verify on their site).** FormBeat's public checkout data listed an active monthly plan at $6 and a yearly plan at $48, with trial variants. Older $25 / year SKUs appear inactive. Confirm at [formbeat.com](https://www.formbeat.com/).

If you are a committed cyclist who wants science-based next-ride guidance from data you already have, FormBeat is a coherent product.

## How Trainingload.ai is a different FormBeat alternative

Trainingload.ai starts from the endurance **plan**, not from a single sport dashboard:

- **Multi-sport training load.** Running, cycling, and triathlon share the same plan vs completed + CTL / ATL / TSB conversation.
- **The plan is the object.** AI review drafts changes to scheduled work after looking at what was completed, not only what the next ride "should" be in isolation.
- **Confirmation is required.** Suggestions stay drafts. That is slower than a daily proposed session, and it is intentional.
- **Shared analysis tools.** FTP, CP, VDOT, VO2 Max, LTHR, and converters are available without an account. Coach and adaptive-plan features use PLUS / PRO AI credits. See [pricing](/pricing).

Honest limits: Trainingload.ai is not as cycling-dashboard-dense as FormBeat (peak-power library, polarization index, ride-native chat shortcuts). If you only ride, and you want tomorrow's suggestion more than a 12-week plan object, FormBeat may still fit better.

## Feature comparison

| Job | FormBeat | Trainingload.ai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary sport | Cycling analytics and AI ride suggestions | Running, cycling, triathlon in one plan loop |
| Load model | Fitness, fatigue, form; optimal-load style guidance | PMC (CTL / ATL / TSB) plus effective VO2max as decision context |
| AI coach | Daily / conversational suggestions from ride history | Plan-linked drafts you confirm |
| Plan object | Calendar and suggested sessions | Adaptive plan that updates after completed work |
| Ingest | Strava, Garmin, file upload | File upload and selected connections; not every vendor pair is live |
| Who should confirm | Athlete accepts or ignores a suggestion | Athlete must confirm material plan edits |

**Bottom line:** Choose FormBeat if the unit of work is *today's ride*. Choose Trainingload.ai if the unit of work is *this week's plan after the rides and runs that actually happened*.

## Who should stay on FormBeat

Stay if you:

- Almost only cycle and already live in Strava / Zwift / ROUVY.
- Want a lightweight AI cycling coach on top of existing ride history.
- Care more about power-curve and aerobic-efficiency trends than about a multi-sport plan object.
- Do not want to maintain a structured plan you have to confirm.

**Ideal FormBeat customer:** a self-coached cyclist who wants fitness / fatigue / form explained in plain language and a next-ride suggestion each morning.

## Who should switch to Trainingload.ai

Switch, or overlap for a block, if you:

- Need a **FormBeat alternative** that still talks about load, but for run + ride weeks.
- Want missed sessions to change the *plan*, not only tomorrow's tip.
- Prefer an AI endurance coach that cannot silently rewrite the calendar.
- Already think in TrainingPeaks / Intervals.icu plan language and want the AI layer on that object.

**Ideal Trainingload.ai customer:** an endurance athlete who uses training load to decide whether the plan should build, recover, or adjust — and wants that decision written back onto the calendar after confirmation.

## Switching from FormBeat

### What transfers

- **Thresholds:** Copy FTP / CP / W' and heart-rate anchors so intensity does not jump.
- **Ride files:** Export FIT, GPX, or TCX. Convert first with the [file converter](/converter) if needed.
- **Event date:** Recreate the goal; do not expect form-prediction curves to match vendor to vendor.

### What needs reconfiguration

- Strava-only automations and FormBeat-specific daily suggestion history do not import.
- Polarization index and some cycling-only charts will not have a 1:1 twin.
- A Trainingload.ai plan is a different object than a stack of daily proposed rides. Build or import a plan, then link completed work.

### Practical first week

1. Keep FormBeat visible until one hard ride has been completed under the new plan.
2. Compare planned vs completed before you accept an AI draft.
3. Read CTL / ATL / TSB with how the last key session actually felt.
4. Confirm one low-risk change; do not rebuild the whole month.

No invented switcher quotes. Overlap is the honest migration path.
