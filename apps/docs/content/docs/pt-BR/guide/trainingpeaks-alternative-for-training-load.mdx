# TrainingPeaks alternative for training load

**TL;DR:** TrainingPeaks is the established endurance calendar, coach marketplace, and Premium PMC platform. Trainingload.ai is a TrainingPeaks alternative when you want an **AI coach** to draft the next plan change from **completed workouts + training load**, then wait for you to confirm. Keep TrainingPeaks if you need a human coach workflow, a large plan library, or TrainingPeaks Virtual.

This page is for athletes searching `trainingpeaks alternative` because they already understand calendars and TSS, and now want the plan to move when execution and load disagree.

## Why people look for a TrainingPeaks alternative

Common reasons are practical, not ideological:

- Premium is where PMC (CTL / ATL / TSB), deeper analysis, and flexible planning live. Basic is useful for logging; it is not the full training-load product.
- A purchased or coach-built plan can stay static after missed sessions, travel, or a spike in ATL.
- Self-coached athletes often want a second opinion that is cheaper than a coach seat, without giving up confirmation control.
- Some athletes want the same load conversation for running and cycling without buying the whole TrainingPeaks ecosystem.

Those are fair reasons. They do not make TrainingPeaks a weak product.

## Where TrainingPeaks is especially strong

Be precise about what you would give up:

- **Coach ecosystem.** Coach accounts, athlete seats, comments, and a marketplace are the core business. Trainingload.ai does not replace that CRM.
- **Calendar and device sync.** Planned workouts to Garmin and other devices, plus a mature drag-and-drop week view, are table stakes in that product.
- **PMC as an industry language.** Fitness, Fatigue, and Form on CTL / ATL / TSB is what many coaches already speak. Premium documents this clearly.
- **Plan inventory and indoor riding.** Structured plan purchases and TrainingPeaks Virtual sit outside Trainingload.ai's scope.
- **Public athlete pricing (check their site).** As of September 2026, TrainingPeaks listed Premium around $19.95 per month or $134.99 per year, with a 14-day Premium trial. Coach billing is separate. Always verify [TrainingPeaks athlete pricing](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/pricing/for-athletes/).

If those strengths are the job, stay.

## How Trainingload.ai is a different TrainingPeaks alternative

Trainingload.ai is not a smaller TrainingPeaks. The product loop is:

**Plan → completed workouts → training load → AI review → confirmed adjustment → updated plan.**

That is the opposite of "analyze in one tab, rewrite the calendar by hand in another."

- **Plan vs completed first.** Before CTL or TSB become a decision, the system asks what was planned, what was finished, skipped, or left unlinked.
- **Load as evidence, not a scoreboard.** CTL, ATL, TSB, and response trends (including effective VO2max) sit next to the active plan. They do not replace it.
- **AI coach as a draft.** Suggested workout changes stay confirmable. Nothing important should hit the calendar only because a model was confident.
- **Free analysis tools without an account.** FTP, CP, VDOT, VO2 Max, LTHR, zones, and file conversion can be used before you subscribe. AI coach and adaptive plan personalization use PLUS / PRO AI credits. See [pricing](/pricing).

Honest limits: Trainingload.ai does not offer a coach marketplace, coach-paid Premium seats, or TrainingPeaks Virtual. Direct account-to-account sync is not complete for every provider. Export FIT / GPX / TCX if you need a clean first week.

| Job | TrainingPeaks | Trainingload.ai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training load / PMC | Premium PMC with Fitness, Fatigue, Form | PMC signals plus plan-linked decisions and effective VO2max trend |
| Planning | Coach plans, purchased plans, Premium calendar tools | Adaptive plan generation tied to completed work and load |
| AI coach | Not the product center; human coaches are | Built-in draft review you confirm |
| Coach marketplace | Core strength | Not offered |
| Device / indoor | Mature sync; TrainingPeaks Virtual | Upload and selected connections; no Virtual indoor product |

**Bottom line:** Choose TrainingPeaks if the calendar and coach relationship *are* the product. Choose Trainingload.ai if the missing piece is an AI endurance coach that will not change next Tuesday until you say so.

## Who should stay on TrainingPeaks

Stay if you:

- Work with a coach who already lives in TrainingPeaks.
- Need Premium PMC plus a large structured-plan catalog more than an adjustment loop.
- Depend on TrainingPeaks Virtual or a specific coach-to-watch workflow.
- Only need a log and are happy on Basic.

**Ideal TrainingPeaks customer:** a coached or plan-following athlete who wants the industry-standard calendar and is willing to pay Premium for PMC and planning depth.

## Who should switch to Trainingload.ai

Switch, or run both for a block, if you:

- Are self-coached and want a **training load app** that proposes the next change after missed work or rising ATL.
- Care more about **plan vs completed** than about a marketplace.
- Want AI review without giving the model write-access to the week.
- Train across running and cycling and want one load conversation.

**Ideal Trainingload.ai customer:** a self-coached runner or cyclist who already understands TSS / PMC language and wants the plan to move only after evidence plus confirmation.

## Switching from TrainingPeaks

### What transfers

- **Thresholds:** Copy FTP, CP, LTHR, and pace anchors by hand so load does not jump.
- **Recent activities:** Export FIT, GPX, or TCX and upload. Use the [FIT / GPX / TCX converter](/converter) if you need a different file shape first.
- **Race dates and weekly structure:** Recreate the goal and key sessions; do not expect a one-click coach-plan clone.

### What needs reconfiguration

- Device destinations and coach comments do not move.
- Structured workout libraries and Virtual courses stay in TrainingPeaks.
- Historical PMC will not be pixel-identical across vendors. Use one week of overlapping data before you trust a new TSB number.

### Practical first week

1. Keep both accounts until one key session has been completed and reviewed.
2. Compare plan vs completed before you touch intensity.
3. Read CTL / ATL / TSB with the response trend, not in isolation.
4. Apply **one** conservative AI draft and validate on the next key workout.

We do not offer white-glove migration, and we do not invent switcher testimonials. The conservative path is overlap, not a hard cutover.
