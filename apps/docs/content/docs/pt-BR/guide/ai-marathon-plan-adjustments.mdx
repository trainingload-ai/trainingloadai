# AI Marathon Plan Adjustments

AI adjustments are strongest when they are conservative, explicit, and reviewable.

## Trigger checklist

Use AI adjustment drafts when one or more are true:

- Key sessions are repeatedly missed.
- ATL is climbing faster than expected.
- TSB remains strongly negative near quality days.
- effective VO2max trend stalls across 10-14 days.

## Adjustment order

Use this sequence:

1. Keep training frequency stable.
2. Modify one major variable first (volume or intensity).
3. Protect long run and marathon-pace quality.
4. Re-evaluate after next key workout.

## Confirmation standard

Before applying AI changes, confirm:

- Why the change was proposed.
- What exactly changes in the session.
- Which risk is reduced.
- What metric will validate the change.

## Common safe edits

- Replace one threshold run with easy aerobic.
- Shorten one quality session but keep structure.
- Shift a key workout by 24-48 hours.
