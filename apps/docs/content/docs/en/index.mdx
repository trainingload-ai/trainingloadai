## One complete training loop

Trainingload.ai is most useful when the parts stay connected:

1. Set your profile, thresholds, zones, units, and time zone.
2. Connect one reliable data source or upload an activity file.
3. Create or choose a plan and check the upcoming schedule.
4. Follow today's workout, or record why training changed.
5. Sync, record, or upload the completed activity.
6. Verify processing, load, and the plan-to-activity link.
7. Review the session, current state, and recent trend together.
8. Keep or adjust future training, then review the week and cycle again.
