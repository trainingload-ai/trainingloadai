## From setup to training review [#one-complete-training-loop]

Use these steps to set up your account, record your training and explore the analysis:

1. Set your profile, time zone, units, thresholds and training zones.
2. Connect a training platform or upload files to import past activities.
3. If you want to follow a plan, create or choose one and schedule its workouts.
4. Check the day's schedule, then sync or record the activity after training.
5. Open activity details to view charts and training load, and link the activity to its planned workout.
6. Review the week's training and load trends. You can also ask AI Coach questions before deciding whether to adjust future sessions.
