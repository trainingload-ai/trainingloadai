# TrainingPeaks alternative for training load

If you use TrainingPeaks, start by comparing the tasks you do most: following a coach's schedule, reviewing load, or adjusting next week's workouts after seeing what you completed.

TrainingPeaks offers a training calendar, coach collaboration, a plan marketplace, and Virtual indoor training. Check the [TrainingPeaks website](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/) and [athlete pricing](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/pricing/for-athletes/) for current features and plans.

## Managing training in Trainingload.ai

- Compare planned workouts with recorded activities in the calendar, including skipped or unlinked sessions.
- Review PMC and performance trends alongside recent training and recovery.
- Ask AI Coach to explain an activity or suggest a workout adjustment, then inspect the change before applying it.
- Create a plan from an official template, the manual editor, or an AI Coach draft.

Trainingload.ai does not provide the TrainingPeaks coach marketplace or Virtual. If you already depend on a coach or device workflow, check which parts still need the original platform.

## Try one week of training

1. [Import recent activities](/docs/guide/connect-data) and check sources, dates, and thresholds.
2. [Create a plan draft](/docs/guide/create-training-plan) for your upcoming workouts.
3. Link one completed activity in [Calendar](/docs/guide/calendar) and compare expected with actual load.
4. Open [AI Coach](/docs/guide/ai-coach) from the relevant activity or plan. Review one answer before deciding whether to apply an adjustment.

Keep the original records during the trial. Load methods, thresholds, and date ranges can differ between platforms, so historical curves may not match exactly.

## Before moving your records

- Activity-file upload is not a complete account migration.
- Check plans, competitions, and notes separately; do not assume they transfer with an activity.
- Compare results using your existing FTP, threshold heart rate, and pace settings before changing them.
- See [Trainingload.ai pricing](/pricing) for account access and AI usage.

Continue with the [training analysis workflow](/docs/guide/training-analysis-workflow), or [open the Demo](https://demo.trainingload.ai/en) to browse examples first.
