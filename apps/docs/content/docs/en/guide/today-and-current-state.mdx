# Use Today's Advice and Current Status

Use **Home** on Web or **Today** in the mobile app to orient your day. Read the recommendation with the current plan, recorded activity and load context, then choose the action that fits what actually happened.

[Open the Demo home page](https://demo.trainingload.ai/en). Demo supports browsing; actions that save workouts, reminders or plan changes require your own account. The screenshots are illustrative examples in the real Web app. Mobile uses its own sheets and recording controls.

## Before you begin

Check the account timezone, active plan and whether recent activities have finished processing. For AI features, review the data-sharing choice and the AI configuration or access shown by your deployment. A plan is useful context when present; a day without a scheduled workout has a different starting point.

## 1. Read the day before taking an action

On Home, check the date and current plan. Locate today's scheduled workouts, the recent activity and the current-state or weekly-load section. A workout listed for today is an intended session. A recorded activity is evidence of training that took place.

If the activity has not synchronized yet, resolve that state before interpreting a recommendation that appears to omit it. Follow [Connect Training Data](/docs/guide/connect-data) when needed.

## 2. Open the advice and inspect its evidence

Select the advice card to open its detail sheet. Read the complete recommendation, any uncertainty, and **Why this recommendation** when available. The available buttons depend on today's recommendation and schedule.

- An existing planned session can offer an action to open that workout.
- A missing-data situation can offer a connection, import or activity-review entry.
- A question or decision can open the corresponding Coach flow.
- A proposed independent workout or reminder appears only when offered for that suggestion.

Opening the sheet does not itself create a workout or apply a plan change. If the advice is replaced after new activity or plan information arrives, read the updated version before acting.

## 3. Follow the action for this situation

### Open an existing workout

Use the offered workout action, then check its date, sport, structure, notes and status. For completed training, check the activity association rather than relying only on the workout's completion label.

In the mobile app, supported planned sessions can enter endurance recording or strength execution. Use [Mobile Recording](/docs/guide/mobile-recording) or [Mobile Plans and Strength](/docs/guide/mobile-plans-and-strength) for the relevant execution flow.

### Review an offered independent workout

When the sheet contains **Suggested workout**, open its preview and inspect the sport, duration, intensity and structure. If you choose its add or schedule action, it creates an independent workout for today. Check the resulting daily-workout view. The existing training plan remains a separate schedule.

You may be unable to add the workout if the suggestion has expired, a workout is already scheduled, or the available training window has ended. Refresh the suggestion and check today's schedule before trying again.

### Set an offered reminder

On Web, choose the reminder action, select **Remind me at**, then **Schedule**. Use a time within the range the interface accepts and wait for success feedback. A reminder prompts a later review; it does not complete training.

Mobile offers supported reminder offsets. Notification delivery also depends on the app's notification and device-permission settings. Check the available reminder options instead of assuming every day offers the same times.

### Ask Coach or review a pending decision

Select **Coach** to discuss the recommendation. Check the question and referenced activities before sending. Open a pending adjustment or assessment to see which workout it concerns, what information is needed, and what confirming it will do.

Opening an activity or plan is navigation. Creating a workout, saving a reminder or confirming a proposed adjustment changes account state. Check the result of each action separately.

## 4. Record what actually happened

After training, synchronize or import the activity, wait for processing and associate it with the intended scheduled session. Compare planned content with the recorded duration, distance, intensity and available load.

If a workout was moved, shortened, skipped or completed outside the plan, preserve that context. Use the [Calendar guide](/docs/guide/calendar) and [Manage an Active Plan](/docs/guide/manage-active-plan) to update the relevant record.

## 5. Use mobile feedback and current-state cards

The mobile advice sheet can offer feedback for resting today, training elsewhere, pending synchronization or reviewing later. This supplies context for the advice. A completed activity still needs to be recorded and synchronized through its normal route.

For mobile current-state customization, open the settings control beside **Current Status**, add or remove supported metrics, arrange the selected cards and finish editing. Open a card for its detail and use its documentation entry to understand the metric.

For load and capability histories, continue with [PMC](/docs/guide/pmc-forecast) and [VO2Max](/docs/guide/effective-vo2max-for-endurance). A current card, a historical point and a future estimate describe different evidence.

## 6. Refresh after changes

The mobile Today view supports pull-to-refresh. After importing activity or changing a plan on another device, refresh and verify the date, status and association before continuing an edit. The Web view can also update when returning to it; inspect the latest visible content.

## Check the result

You should be able to identify the day's planned session, the recorded activity, the advice's supporting context and the effect of the action you selected. Confirm that a saved workout, association or reminder appears in the expected place.

## Common questions

### Why is today's advice missing or unavailable?

Check data-sharing choices, AI configuration or access, request status and available training evidence. Use an explicit error or retry state instead of treating a loading or fallback display as a fresh generated recommendation.

### Why did the advice change?

New activity, a changed plan, updated context or expiry can cause it to refresh. Recheck the current version before applying a previous proposal.

### Why is there no workout action?

Actions depend on the suggestion and current evidence. Verify the active plan, scheduled date and workout status, then use the calendar or plan to locate the intended session.

### Why does the recommendation omit a workout I completed?

Check synchronization, activity processing, local date and plan association. In mobile, provide the relevant feedback while the missing record is being resolved.

### Can I use the advice as an automatic plan change?

Read the offered action and verify its result. A recommendation, an independent workout and a proposed plan adjustment have different effects. Follow [AI Coach](/docs/guide/ai-coach) for review and confirmation.
