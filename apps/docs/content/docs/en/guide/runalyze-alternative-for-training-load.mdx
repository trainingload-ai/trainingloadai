# Runalyze alternative for training load

If you use Runalyze to review running performance, compare both tools using the same recent activities and the decisions you need to make about upcoming training.

Runalyze provides effective VO2max, race predictions, training load, and activity statistics. See its [official feature overview](https://runalyze.com/help/article/features?_locale=en) for methods and current features.

## Reviewing training in Trainingload.ai

- [Open Activity Detail](/docs/guide/activity-detail) to check the recorded workout first.
- [Review effective VO2max history](/docs/guide/effective-vo2max-for-endurance) to see how recent estimates changed.
- [Open PMC](/docs/guide/pmc-forecast) and interpret load alongside your training schedule.
- Link an activity to its planned workout, then ask [AI Coach](/docs/guide/ai-coach) to review completion or suggest an adjustment.

## Compare using your own records

1. Export supported FIT, GPX, or TCX files from the source platform, or use a [platform connection](/docs/guide/connect-data).
2. Import a recent training block and check dates, sources, and thresholds.
3. Choose one familiar workout and compare its recorded duration, distance, heart rate, and pace.
4. Then review load and fitness estimates. One differing estimate does not establish a change in fitness.

## What needs setting up again

Platforms can use different valid samples, corrections, and thresholds. Effective VO2max and load histories may not match exactly. Uploading activities also does not automatically migrate Runalyze tags, equipment, custom settings, or every historical statistic.

Keep the original records while trying a plan-versus-activity review. Then decide whether to migrate or use both tools. To see the interface first, [open the Demo](https://demo.trainingload.ai/en).
