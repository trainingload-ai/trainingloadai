# Import Your First Activity [#get-started-with-your-first-activity]

Import a recording from your watch, bike computer or training platform to see its activity details, charts and training load in Trainingload.ai. This guide walks through platform sync and file uploads in the web app.

## Before you begin

[Open Trainingload.ai](https://app.trainingload.ai/en) and sign in to your own account. If you self-host, open your own instance instead. To see the interface first, [browse the Demo](https://demo.trainingload.ai/en).

## 1. Choose how to import [#2-choose-one-route-for-the-first-activity]

| Sync method | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Sync from a third-party training platform (Strava, Garmin, Intervals.icu) | [Connect the platform](#connect-a-training-platform) |
| Sync from activity files (FIT, TCX, GPX) | [Upload the file](#upload-a-workout-file) |
| Sync from your phone | [Read the phone sync guide](/docs/guide/account-permissions-and-usage#2-review-mobile-permissions-and-data-sources) |

For self-hosting, see [Configuration](/docs/self-host/configuration) for supported connections. You can also use the file-upload steps below.

## 2. Import your recording [#3-import-and-wait-for-processing]

### Option 1: Connect a training platform [#connect-a-training-platform]

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner, then scroll down to **Third Party Connections**.
2. Choose the platform that holds your recordings and follow the dialog to connect it.
3. Use its initial-import or manual-sync option if needed.
4. Open **Activities** in the sidebar to find the synced recordings.

Each platform supports a different history range. See [Connect Strava, Garmin and other platforms](/docs/guide/connect-third-party-platforms) for its connection and history-import steps.

### Option 2: Upload a workout file [#upload-a-workout-file]

Export a FIT, TCX or GPX file from your watch, bike computer or training platform, then:

1. Open **Activities** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Choose activity files** to open the import dialog.

<DocsScreenshot
  id="activity-upload-location"
  title="Find the upload button in Activities"
  description="Open Activities and select Choose activity files."

3. Click the file-selection area and choose your files, or drag them into the dialog.
4. Wait for the file check to finish, then select **Upload**.
5. Wait for the import to finish in the progress dialog, then open the new recording from Activities.

If you see a duplicate warning, you can keep the existing recording rather than import the same workout twice. See [Upload Activity Files](/docs/guide/upload-activity-files) for file formats, duplicate choices and failed imports.

## 3. Open your imported activity [#4-verify-the-first-recorded-result]

Find the workout by date in **Activities** and open its details. You can see duration, distance, training load, and the charts and route supported by the recording. See [Activity Details](/docs/guide/activity-detail) for a tour of the page.

A newly imported activity may still be processing. Wait for analysis to finish before viewing the full results. Heart rate, power and route charts need the corresponding recorded data; a recording without GPS will not show a route.

## Common questions

### I connected a platform but cannot see my activities

Check that the platform is connected and review the date filters in Activities. Past workouts may need a separate history import. Follow the platform's options, or export a workout file and upload it to Trainingload.ai.

### My activity has no heart rate or route

Wait for processing to finish. If the original file has no heart rate, power or GPS data, the corresponding charts will not appear. If the source platform shows those measurements, try exporting the original activity file and uploading it again.

### The activity appears on the wrong date

Check your account time zone and the recording's start time. See [Profile, Thresholds and Zones](/docs/guide/profile-thresholds-zones) for time zone and unit settings.

### Why can I not upload in Demo?

Demo is for browsing sample data. To import your own recordings, [open the main app](https://app.trainingload.ai/en) and sign in to your own account.
