# Intervals.icu alternative for training load

If you already analyze training in Intervals.icu, decide whether you want to replace a tool or keep that workflow while trying Trainingload.ai for planning and review.

Intervals.icu offers activity timelines, power and heart-rate charts, interval analysis, and custom charts. See its [activity analysis overview](https://www.intervals.icu/features/analyze/) for current features.

## What to try in Trainingload.ai

- Link completed activities to planned workouts and compare the original schedule with what happened.
- Review the activity timeline, PMC, and performance trends alongside the plan.
- Open AI Coach from an activity or plan to review an answer or inspect a suggested workout change.

Start with [Activity Detail](/docs/guide/activity-detail), [Timeline](/docs/guide/activity-timeline), and [Manage Plan Status](/docs/planning/plan-lifecycle). Compare the training tasks you actually use.

## Connect your Intervals.icu records

1. For Cloud, follow the [platform connection guide](/docs/guide/connect-third-party-platforms#connect-intervalsicu). For self-hosting, follow the [API key settings](/docs/self-host/configuration#connect-intervalsicu).
2. Choose the sync directions you need. Decide which platform maintains zones and plans before allowing both to update the same schedule.
3. Sync one known activity and check its date, duration, source, and sensor data.
4. Link it to a workout in [Calendar](/docs/guide/calendar), then compare planned and actual load.

## Comparing results

The same activity can have different load or zone distributions across platforms. Compare the original record, thresholds, and load-source settings before interpreting the difference. Connecting activities does not automatically copy every custom chart, field, or plan.

Keep both platforms while trying one complete import, workout-linking, and review sequence. Then decide which setup fits your needs. To see the interface first, [open the Demo](https://demo.trainingload.ai/en).
