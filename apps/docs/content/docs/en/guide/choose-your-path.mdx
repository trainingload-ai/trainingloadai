# Choose Your Path

You do not need to configure every metric before Trainingload.ai becomes useful. Choose the outcome you want first, finish that path, and return for deeper setup later.

## I want to understand my recent training

Choose this path if you already have completed workouts on another platform or as FIT, TCX, or GPX files.

1. Complete [Getting Started](/docs/guide/getting-started).
2. [Connect a provider](/docs/guide/integrations) or [upload activity files](/docs/guide/upload-activity-files).
3. Open [Activity Detail](/docs/guide/activity-detail) and confirm that time, distance, sport, and available sensor data look correct.
4. Use the [Dashboard](/docs/guide/dashboard) and [training analysis workflow](/docs/guide/training-analysis-workflow) to interpret the trend.

**You are done when:** at least one activity has finished processing and its charts match the source data you expected.

## I want to follow a training plan

Choose this path if the next workout matters more than historical analysis.

1. Check your [profile, thresholds, and zones](/docs/guide/profile-thresholds-zones).
2. [Create a training plan](/docs/guide/create-training-plan) manually or with AI.
3. Review the workouts, then activate the plan with the intended start date.
4. Use [Calendar](/docs/guide/calendar) or the Plan page to find today's session.
5. After training, [link the completed activity to the planned workout](/docs/guide/manage-active-plan).

**You are done when:** the plan is active, today's workout is visible, and the first completed activity is linked or ready to link.

## I train mainly on mobile

Use mobile for today's work, recording, quick review, and plan execution. Use web when you want more room for detailed charts or editing a multi-week plan.

1. Read [Web and Mobile](/docs/guide/web-and-mobile) to understand what stays synchronized.
2. Complete the permission checklist in [Mobile Recording](/docs/guide/mobile-recording).
3. Record or import a workout.
4. Open Today, Calendar, Plan, Coach, or Me to continue the same account workflow.

## I want AI to help build or adjust training

AI Coach works best after your profile, recent activities, and plan are available.

1. Add the thresholds you trust; leave unknown values unset rather than guessing.
2. Import enough recent training to show your normal workload.
3. Open [AI Coach](/docs/guide/ai-coach) and state the goal, time horizon, constraints, and available days.
4. Review every plan draft or workout adjustment before applying it.

**You are done when:** the recommendation cites your actual plan or activities and the proposed change is visible before it is saved.

## I create strength workouts

1. Create or open a manual plan.
2. Choose **Strength Training** in the workout editor.
3. Follow [Strength Workouts](/docs/planning/strength-workouts) to add exercises, work sets, load, effort, and rest.
4. Save and verify the preview before using or exporting the session.

## If you are unsure

Start with [Getting Started](/docs/guide/getting-started). It follows the smallest complete loop: profile → completed activity → training load → plan → review.
