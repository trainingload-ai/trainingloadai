# Notifications and Review Automation

Use notifications to check import results and completed reviews. With review automation enabled, eligible daily, weekly, or monthly reviews can be generated without requesting each one manually.

## Before you enable automation

- Set the correct time zone for daily, weekly, and monthly boundaries.
- Import activities consistently and keep the active plan current.
- Link completed activities when plan execution should be reviewed.
- Check account entitlement and AI usage availability.

## Notification types

Notifications can include general system messages, updates to continuous training plans, and completed training reviews. Import dialogs may also tell you that a notification will arrive when a background batch completes, so you do not need to keep the dialog open.

From Notifications you can:

- Open the related review or destination.
- Mark one notification as read.
- Mark all notifications as read.

Marking a notification read does not delete the underlying activity, plan, or coach session.

## Read a training review

Daily reviews list the previous day's activities, weekly reviews summarize the last completed week by sport, and monthly reviews group the last completed calendar month by week. When available, coach insights highlight what went well, what to watch, and one focus for the next period. Select a referenced activity to check the original record.

Weekly reviews also show training days, average session duration, and training load when available. Multiple sessions on the same day count as one training day.

Sleep duration, HRV, and resting heart rate appear when records are available. Daily reviews use the following day's recovery records and your recent baseline when it can be calculated. Weekly and monthly reviews show how many days were recorded and compare with the previous period when the readings are comparable.

On web, paired bars compare the two periods. The tables retain the values and show percentage change for session count, duration, and comparable recovery metrics when the previous value is above zero.

## Configure review automation

Open **Account → AI Review Automation** on web or the equivalent account panel on mobile.

The available switches can include:

- Activity review.
- Daily review.
- Weekly review.
- Monthly review.
- Plan review.

Automation availability depends on account entitlement and AI usage limits. Enabling a switch authorizes that review category; it does not guarantee useful output when required activity or plan data is missing.

## Make sure the review has your training records

Before relying on automation:

1. Confirm time zone, because daily and weekly boundaries depend on it.
2. Import completed activities consistently.
3. Keep the active plan current.
4. Link completed activities to planned workouts.
5. Add session RPE or current-state context when relevant.

## Manually trigger a review

Where the product exposes manual review controls, you can request a review for the last completed day, week, or month without changing the automation schedule. Review jobs run in the background. Wait for completion before submitting another request.

## Verify success

- Saved switches remain enabled after reopening settings.
- A completed review produces a notification or coach entry.
- Opening the notification navigates to the expected review.
- The review references the correct time window, plan, or activity.

## Troubleshooting

If a review does not appear, check entitlement, usage, time zone, data availability, and whether the background job is still running. For structured action failures, open [Troubleshooting](/docs/guide/troubleshooting).

Requesting the review again will not supply missing activities and may consume AI usage. Check that the activities have been imported and processed. If a review is already being generated, wait for its result.
