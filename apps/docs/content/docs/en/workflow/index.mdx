# Complete Training Workflow

The product is not a collection of independent charts. A trustworthy decision comes from connecting the plan, the work that actually happened, the data quality behind it, and the athlete's current context.

## The loop

| Stage | Question | Primary guide | Finished when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Calibrate | Are units, time zone, thresholds, and zones usable? | [Profile, thresholds, and zones](/docs/guide/profile-thresholds-zones) | The profile matches the sport and current ability |
| 2. Connect | Which source supplies activities and health data? | [Connect data](/docs/guide/connect-data) | At least one activity reaches a Ready state |
| 3. Plan | What is the current goal and schedule? | [Create a training plan](/docs/guide/create-training-plan) | A reviewed plan is active or ready to activate |
| 4. Execute | What should happen today? | [Today and current state](/docs/guide/today-and-current-state) | The athlete knows the planned session and constraints |
| 5. Capture | What actually happened? | [Mobile recording and file upload](/docs/guide/mobile-recording) | A completed activity is saved or imported |
| 6. Reconcile | Does the activity belong to a planned workout? | [Activity feed and search](/docs/guide/activity-feed-search) | Plan and actual states are correctly linked or intentionally separate |
| 7. Review | What do the activity and recent trend support? | [Activity detail](/docs/guide/activity-detail) | Source, load, charts, and context support the interpretation |
| 8. Decide | Should future training stay the same? | [AI Coach](/docs/guide/ai-coach) | Any proposed change is explicit and reviewed |
| 9. Repeat | Is the week and cycle still moving toward the goal? | [Weekly review](/docs/workflow/weekly-review) | The next review point and next key session are clear |

## Start with the main path

Use [the first end-to-end training cycle](/docs/workflow/first-training-cycle) when setting up a new account or checking whether the whole product flow is connected.

Then use the scenario guides when reality differs from the plan:

- [Skipped, shortened, replaced, or unplanned training](/docs/workflow/when-training-changes)
- [Missing activities, load, or recovery data](/docs/workflow/data-and-load-problems)
- [Weekly plan-versus-actual review](/docs/workflow/weekly-review)

## Keep observation and decision separate

An activity, chart, or AI message is evidence—not an automatic plan change. First confirm the source and time range, then state the interpretation, and only then decide whether future training should change.
