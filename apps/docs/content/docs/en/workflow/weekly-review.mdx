# Weekly Training Review

## Outcome

Finish a 15–20 minute review with one written conclusion, at most one primary adjustment, and a date or workout for checking whether the decision helped.

## 1. Check data completeness

Open [Activities](/docs/guide/activity-feed-search). Resolve processing failures, duplicates, and accidental plan links before reading weekly totals.

## 2. Compare plan and actual

Use [Calendar](/docs/guide/calendar) and the current plan. Count completed, shortened, skipped, replaced, and unplanned sessions. Do not reduce the review to completion percentage alone.

## 3. Review training dose

Use [Dashboard](/docs/guide/dashboard) and [Training load](/docs/metrics/training-load). Keep sport and time-range filters consistent. Look for the sessions that explain the change, not only the aggregate number.

## 4. Add response and context

Review sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, session RPE, soreness, and subjective fatigue when available. Missing recovery data reduces confidence; it does not prove recovery was good or bad.

## 5. Decide the next action

Choose one:

- Keep the plan unchanged.
- Move one session.
- Reduce one duration or intensity target.
- Replace one session with recovery work.
- Escalate for human or medical review when the question exceeds training guidance.

## Verify the review

Write down the evidence, the decision, the exact future session affected, and the next review point. If using [AI Coach](/docs/guide/ai-coach), confirm that its answer cites the same time range and evidence.
