# Self-hosting

Self-hosting lets you use Trainingload.ai on your own computer or server. Your activities, training plans, and uploaded files stay on your device, and you review and manage training in a browser.

One self-hosted instance is for one person. You look after the device, software updates, and backups. If you would prefer to open the app and get started directly, read [Self-hosted vs Cloud](/docs/self-host/self-host-vs-cloud) first.

## What you can do

- Import activities and review their details, timeline, and training load.
- Arrange training in the calendar and compare planned with completed workouts.
- Upload FIT, GPX, or TCX files, or connect Intervals.icu and Garmin Personal API with personal keys.
- Configure your own AI service to use AI Coach, or leave AI off.

## What to prepare before installing

- A device that meets the [installation requirements](/docs/self-host/installation), with Docker Compose installed.
- Enough disk space for your training data and uploaded files.
- A separate place to keep backups so you can recover if the device fails.

Use an official, versioned, signed release bundle and follow the guide to verify it before installing. You do not need to build the source or assemble containers yourself. Docker Compose is the currently supported installation method.

## Where your data goes

Activities, plans, and uploaded files are saved on your device. Connecting a training platform sends or receives data according to the sync options you choose. Using an external AI service sends the training information needed for that request to your chosen provider.

AI is off by default. To enable it, follow the [configuration guide](/docs/self-host/configuration) and enter your own provider API key. That provider charges for API usage.
