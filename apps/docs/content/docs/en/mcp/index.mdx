# TrainingLoad MCP

TrainingLoad MCP lets an AI client answer questions with your own training data. It is a goal-oriented read-only product surface rather than a raw collection of endpoints: a client can establish your current state, compare training blocks, inspect plan execution, review fitness trends, or diagnose sync readiness, then tie each conclusion to returned evidence.

**Server URL:** `https://api.trainingload.ai/mcp`

No API key is required. The client opens TrainingLoad OAuth, where you review the client name and requested scopes. You can revoke access later from your account.

## What the 24 read-only tools cover

| Question | Primary capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| What is my training state now? | Training snapshot, readiness indicators, load series |
| What changed between two training blocks? | Period comparison, sport breakdown, activity statistics, and consistency |
| Is my running, cycling, or swimming fitness changing? | Fitness trends, training zones, personal bests |
| How closely am I following my plan? | Plan discovery and summary, planned workouts, workout detail, status-based adherence |
| What training did I complete? | Activity list, privacy-reduced detail, time in zones, and lap/split summaries |
| What competitions are ahead? | Competition list and user-scoped event detail |
| Why is a provider not syncing? | Connection and sync readiness |
| Has my wellness context changed? | Separately authorized daily health summary |

See [Tool reference](/docs/mcp/tools) for every field and scope. Clients can also discover three built-in [resources and prompts](/docs/mcp/resources-and-prompts) for metric interpretation, safety boundaries, and repeatable analysis workflows.

## How a transparent answer is produced

For “Why does this week feel harder than last week?”, a client should:

1. Call `get_training_snapshot` for one consistent current-state view.
2. Call `compare_training_periods` or `get_training_load` to verify the week-over-week difference.
3. Call `list_planned_workouts` only if the upcoming schedule matters.
4. State the date range, supporting facts, and missing data without turning correlation into a medical conclusion.

This aggregate-first, detail-on-demand pattern is more reliable and easier to verify than a chain of unrelated low-level calls.

## Product boundaries

- **Read-only:** no activity, plan, competition, profile, integration, or account mutations.
- **Current athlete only:** every identifier and query is bound to the OAuth user.
- **Reduced data:** no email, internal user ID, precise GPS, raw sensor stream, third-party credential, or complete FIT data.
- **Bounded access:** time series and lists enforce windows, result limits, or opaque cursors.
- **Health isolation:** `health:read` is excluded from the default grant.
- **Verifiable conclusions:** names, descriptions, and notes are untrusted data, never model instructions.
