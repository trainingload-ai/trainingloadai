# TrainingLoad MCP

Connect your training data to ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, or another compatible AI client with TrainingLoad MCP. Ask about recent load, plan completion, performance trends, or sync problems without copying individual activity records. MCP reads data; it does not change your training schedule.

**Server URL:** `https://api.trainingload.ai/mcp`

No API key is required. The client opens TrainingLoad OAuth, where you review the client name and requested scopes. You can revoke access later from your account.

## Training information you can read

| Question | Primary capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| What is my training state now? | Training snapshot, readiness indicators, load series |
| What changed between two training blocks? | Period comparison, sport breakdown, activity statistics, and consistency |
| Is my running, cycling, or swimming fitness changing? | Fitness trends, training zones, personal bests |
| How closely am I following my plan? | Plan discovery and summary, planned workouts, workout detail, status-based adherence |
| What training did I complete? | Activity list, privacy-reduced detail, time in zones, and lap/split summaries |
| What competitions are ahead? | Competition list and user-scoped event detail |
| Why is a provider not syncing? | Connection and sync readiness |
| Has my wellness context changed? | Separately authorized daily health summary |

See [Tool reference](/docs/mcp/tools) for every field and scope. Clients can also discover three built-in [resources and prompts](/docs/mcp/resources-and-prompts) for metric interpretation, safety boundaries, and repeatable analysis workflows.

## Questions to try

- “Compare this week's volume and load with last week. Which activities explain the main changes?”
- “How much of this week's plan did I complete, and which workouts still have no linked activity?”
- “How have my recent running metrics changed? Which dates lack comparable data?”

When reading an answer, check its date range, referenced activities, and missing data. Trends can support a training discussion; they do not establish a medical conclusion.

## Limits to understand before connecting

- **Read-only:** clients cannot change activities, plans, competitions, profiles, connections, or accounts.
- **Your data only:** clients can query only records belonging to your authorized account.
- **Training summaries:** tools do not provide precise GPS, raw sensor streams, provider credentials, or complete FIT files.
- **Separate health permission:** `health:read` is not included by default.
- **Revocable access:** remove a client's permission from your account when you no longer use it.
