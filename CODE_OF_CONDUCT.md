# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Trainingload.ai community to be safe, respectful, and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when discussing different goals, experiences, and technical opinions.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project and the problem being solved.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never publish credentials, private messages, health information, activity files, GPS routes, or other personal data without explicit permission.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, correct them, and avoid repeating harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or unwanted attention.
- Insults, trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information or attempting to identify an athlete from shared data.
- Sharing malicious code, exploitation instructions, credentials, or private data through public project channels.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation, community channels, and public spaces where someone officially represents Trainingload.ai.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@trainingload.ai`. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive evidence or personal information. Project maintainers will review reports as privately and fairly as possible and may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
