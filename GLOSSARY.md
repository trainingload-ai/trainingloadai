# Training domain

Training plans, planned workouts, and completed activities describe different parts of an athlete's training.

## Language

**Training plan**:
A set of planned workouts organized toward an athlete's training goal over a period of time. A workout does not need a training plan to exist.

**Planned workout**:
A training session the athlete intends to perform on a date. It may contain structured intervals or only a name, sport, notes, and an optional duration.

**Independent workout**:
A planned workout that does not belong to a training plan. It remains a complete training session with its own schedule, completion state, and relationship to a completed activity.

**Calendar adjustment**:
An athlete's proposed set of changes to one or more planned workouts on their calendar, reviewed and confirmed together.

**Calendar adjustment revision**:
A reviewable version of a calendar adjustment evaluated against a particular calendar baseline.

**Activity**:
A record of training that actually took place. An activity can exist without a planned workout.

**Workout–activity link**:
An explicit relationship indicating that an activity represents a planned workout. The planned workout can be independent or belong to a training plan.

**Inferred workout link**:
A relationship inferred from recorded activity and workout evidence. The athlete can correct or remove it when it does not represent their intent.

**Recorded workout binding**:
A relationship explicitly established by starting a recording for a chosen workout. It preserves that chosen workout's identity through activity processing.

**Workout matching review**:
An unresolved activity-to-workout relationship whose evidence is insufficient or ambiguous. The coach can explain the uncertainty and direct the athlete to associate it manually.
